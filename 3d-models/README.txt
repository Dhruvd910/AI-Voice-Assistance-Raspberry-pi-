Real 3D model files for Liza, for the things she does not build herself
(a lung, a liver, a stomach). The heart, brain, kidney and skeleton come
from the 3D Education Engine instead.

Put a .glb, .gltf, .obj, .stl or .ply file here and name it for what it is:
"lungs.glb" answers "show me the lungs in 3D", "human liver.obj" answers
"show me the liver". No restart needed.

Free sources: NIH 3D (3d.nih.gov), Sketchfab (filter: downloadable, CC licence).
Keep files under about 20 MB: the Pi has to load them every time.
