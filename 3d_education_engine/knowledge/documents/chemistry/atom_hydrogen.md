## Definition
Hydrogen (H) has atomic number 1: every hydrogen atom has 1 proton in its nucleus and, when neutral, 1 electron. The electrons are arranged 1 in shells.

## Facts
Hydrogen is the lightest element. Its one electron lets it form one covalent bond; hydrogen gas is H2.
