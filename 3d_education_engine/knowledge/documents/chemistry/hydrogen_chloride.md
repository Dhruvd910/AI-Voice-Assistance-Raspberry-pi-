## Definition
Hydrogen chloride is a molecule of one hydrogen atom and one chlorine atom joined by a single covalent bond, HCl.

## Facts
The bond is polar, because chlorine is more electronegative than hydrogen. Hydrogen chloride dissolves in water to form hydrochloric acid, a strong acid; the stomach makes dilute hydrochloric acid to help digestion and kill microbes.
