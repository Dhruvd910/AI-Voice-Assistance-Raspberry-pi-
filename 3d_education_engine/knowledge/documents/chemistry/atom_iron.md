## Definition
Iron (Fe) has atomic number 26: every iron atom has 26 protons in its nucleus and, when neutral, 26 electrons. The electrons are arranged 2,8,14,2 in shells.

## Facts
Iron is a transition metal (configuration [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s²); it forms Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ ions and is at the centre of each haem group in haemoglobin.
