## Definition
Chlorine (Cl) has atomic number 17: every chlorine atom has 17 protons in its nucleus and, when neutral, 17 electrons. The electrons are arranged 2,8,7 in shells.

## Facts
Chlorine gains one electron to form the chloride ion Cl⁻, or shares one in a covalent bond, as in HCl.
