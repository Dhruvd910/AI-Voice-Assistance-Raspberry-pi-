## Definition
Ethanol is an alcohol with the formula C2H5OH: a two-carbon chain carrying an –OH (hydroxyl) group.

## Facts
It is made by fermentation of sugars by yeast (anaerobic respiration) and is used as a fuel and a solvent. The –OH group makes it able to mix with water.
