## Definition
Nitrogen (N) has atomic number 7: every nitrogen atom has 7 protons in its nucleus and, when neutral, 7 electrons. The electrons are arranged 2,5 in shells.

## Facts
Nitrogen usually forms three covalent bonds, as in ammonia, and has one lone pair.
