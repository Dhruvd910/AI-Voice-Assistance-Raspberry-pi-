## Definition
Nitrogen gas is made of molecules of two nitrogen atoms joined by a triple bond, N2. It makes up about 78% of dry air.

## Facts
The triple bond is very strong, which makes nitrogen gas unreactive; living things cannot use it directly, and nitrogen-fixing bacteria or industrial processes are needed to turn it into compounds such as ammonia.
