## Definition
Oxygen (O) has atomic number 8: every oxygen atom has 8 protons in its nucleus and, when neutral, 8 electrons. The electrons are arranged 2,6 in shells.

## Facts
Oxygen usually forms two covalent bonds, as in water, or gains two electrons to form the oxide ion O²⁻.
