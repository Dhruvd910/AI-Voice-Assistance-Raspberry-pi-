## Definition
Sodium (Na) has atomic number 11: every sodium atom has 11 protons in its nucleus and, when neutral, 11 electrons. The electrons are arranged 2,8,1 in shells.

## Facts
Sodium easily loses its single outer electron to form the Na⁺ ion; it is a very reactive metal.
