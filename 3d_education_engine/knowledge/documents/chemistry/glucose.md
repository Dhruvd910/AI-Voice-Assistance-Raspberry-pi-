## Definition
Glucose is a simple sugar with the formula C6H12O6. In water it exists mostly as a six-membered ring.

## Function
Plants make glucose in photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2, using light energy. Cells break glucose down in respiration to release energy, which is stored in ATP: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O.
