## Definition
Carbon (C) has atomic number 6: every carbon atom has 6 protons in its nucleus and, when neutral, 6 electrons. The electrons are arranged 2,4 in shells.

## Facts
With four outer electrons carbon forms four covalent bonds, so it can build chains and rings: the basis of organic chemistry and of all life.
