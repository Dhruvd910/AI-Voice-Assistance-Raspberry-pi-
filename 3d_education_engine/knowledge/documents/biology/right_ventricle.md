## Definition
The right ventricle is the lower right chamber of the heart. It receives deoxygenated blood from the right atrium through the tricuspid valve.

## Function
It pumps blood through the pulmonary valve into the pulmonary trunk, which divides into the pulmonary arteries leading to the lungs.

## Structure
Its wall is thinner than the left ventricle's (about 3 to 5 mm), because the lungs are close and the pulmonary circulation works at low pressure. It wraps around the front of the left ventricle, so it forms most of the front surface of the heart.

## Misconceptions
A thinner wall does not mean the right ventricle pumps less blood: it pumps the same volume per beat as the left, at lower pressure.

## Questions
Where does blood go after it leaves the right ventricle? Why does the right ventricle not need as thick a wall as the left?
