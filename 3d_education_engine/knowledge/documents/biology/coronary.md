## Definition
The coronary arteries are the blood vessels that supply the heart muscle itself. The right and left coronary arteries branch from the aorta just above the aortic valve and spread over the surface of the heart. Cardiac veins drain the used blood, mostly into the coronary sinus, which empties into the right atrium.

## Function
The heart muscle cannot take oxygen from the blood inside its chambers; it needs its own supply, like any other organ, and it uses oxygen continuously.

## Examples
If a coronary artery is blocked, for example by a blood clot on a fatty plaque, the heart muscle it supplies is starved of oxygen and can die. This is a heart attack (myocardial infarction).

## Misconceptions
A heart attack is not the same as the heart stopping (cardiac arrest), although a heart attack can lead to one.
