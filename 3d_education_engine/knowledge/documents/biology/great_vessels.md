## Definition
The great vessels are the large blood vessels that enter and leave the heart: the aorta, the pulmonary trunk and arteries, the pulmonary veins, and the superior and inferior venae cavae.

## Function
The aorta carries oxygenated blood from the left ventricle to the body; it is the largest artery. The pulmonary trunk carries deoxygenated blood from the right ventricle to the lungs. The pulmonary veins return oxygenated blood from the lungs to the left atrium. The superior vena cava returns blood from the head, neck and arms, and the inferior vena cava from the lower body, both to the right atrium.

## Misconceptions
Not all arteries carry oxygenated blood, and not all veins carry deoxygenated blood. Arteries are defined by carrying blood AWAY from the heart and veins by carrying it TOWARDS the heart. So the pulmonary arteries carry deoxygenated blood and the pulmonary veins carry oxygenated blood.
