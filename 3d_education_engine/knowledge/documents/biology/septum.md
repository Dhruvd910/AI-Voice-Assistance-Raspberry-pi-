## Definition
The septum is the wall that divides the left side of the heart from the right. The interventricular septum lies between the two ventricles; it is mostly muscular, with a small thin membranous part near the top.

## Function
It keeps oxygenated blood on the left separate from deoxygenated blood on the right, and its muscle helps the ventricles contract.

## Facts
In this 3D model (from BodyParts3D) the septum is not a separate piece: it is included in the walls of the ventricles, so it can be explained but not highlighted on its own.

## Examples
A hole in the septum (a septal defect) lets blood leak between the two sides; some babies are born with one.
