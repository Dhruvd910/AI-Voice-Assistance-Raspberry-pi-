## Definition
An animal cell is a eukaryotic cell: its DNA is enclosed in a nucleus, and it contains membrane-bound organelles.

## Structure
The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves. The nucleus holds the DNA; inside it the nucleolus makes ribosomes. Ribosomes make proteins; those on the rough endoplasmic reticulum make proteins that are then transported, and the Golgi apparatus modifies and packages them. Mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration. Lysosomes contain digestive enzymes. Centrioles help organise cell division.

## Misconceptions
Animal cells do not have a cell wall, chloroplasts or a large permanent vacuole; those are features of plant cells. Real cells are not all round: nerve cells, muscle cells and red blood cells have very different shapes.

## Questions
