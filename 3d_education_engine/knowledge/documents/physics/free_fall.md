## Definition
Free fall is motion under gravity alone. Near the Earth's surface every object in free fall accelerates downwards at g ≈ 9.81 m/s², whatever its mass.

## Equations
v = g·t. Distance fallen d = ½·g·t². Time to fall a height h: t = √(2h/g). Speed after falling h: v = √(2gh).

## Misconceptions
Heavier objects do not fall faster in a vacuum: a hammer and a feather dropped on the Moon (by Apollo 15 astronaut David Scott in 1971) landed together. In air, drag slows light, spread-out objects more.
