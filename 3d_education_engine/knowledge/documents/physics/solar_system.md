## Definition
The Solar System is the Sun and everything that orbits it: eight planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune), their moons, dwarf planets such as Pluto, asteroids and comets.

## Facts
The inner four planets are small and rocky; the outer four are giants made mostly of gas or ices. Orbital periods grow with distance: Mercury takes 88 days, Earth 1 year, Neptune about 165 years. The Earth is about 150 million km (1 AU) from the Sun.

## Misconceptions
The planets are far smaller and farther apart than any diagram shows: if the Sun were a football, the Earth would be a peppercorn about 25 m away. Seasons are caused by the tilt of the Earth's axis, not by its distance from the Sun.
