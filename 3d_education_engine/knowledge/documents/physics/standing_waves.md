## Definition
A standing wave forms when two identical waves travel in opposite directions, for example a wave reflected back along a string fixed at both ends.

## Structure
Nodes are points that never move; antinodes are points of maximum vibration, halfway between nodes. For a string of length L fixed at both ends, the n-th harmonic has n loops, wavelength λ = 2L/n and frequency f = n·v/(2L).

## Examples
Guitar and violin strings vibrate in standing waves; pressing a string shortens L and raises the pitch.
