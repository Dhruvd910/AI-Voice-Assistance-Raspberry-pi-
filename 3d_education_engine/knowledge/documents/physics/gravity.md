## Definition
Every mass attracts every other mass. Newton's law of gravitation: F = G·m1·m2 / r², with G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg². The force falls to a quarter when the distance doubles (inverse square law).

## Structure
An orbit is continuous free fall: the planet falls towards the star all the time, but moves sideways fast enough that it keeps missing it. At exactly the right speed the orbit is a circle; a little faster or slower gives an ellipse.

## Equations
Circular orbit speed v = √(GM/r). Kepler's third law: T² ∝ a³; for planets round the Sun, T (years)² = a (AU)³.

## Misconceptions
There is gravity in orbit: astronauts float because they and their spacecraft are falling together, not because gravity is absent. At the height of the International Space Station gravity is still about 90% as strong as on the ground.

## Questions
Why doesn't the Moon fall into the Earth? What happens to the gravitational force when the distance doubles?
