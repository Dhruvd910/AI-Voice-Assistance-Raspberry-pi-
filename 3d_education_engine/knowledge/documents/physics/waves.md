## Definition
A wave transfers energy from place to place without transferring matter. In a transverse wave (light, a wave on a string) the particles vibrate at right angles to the direction the wave travels; in a longitudinal wave (sound) they vibrate along it.

## Equations
Wave speed v = f·λ (frequency × wavelength). Period T = 1/f.

## Misconceptions
The medium does not travel with the wave: each point on the string only moves up and down.

## Questions
A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. How fast does it travel?
