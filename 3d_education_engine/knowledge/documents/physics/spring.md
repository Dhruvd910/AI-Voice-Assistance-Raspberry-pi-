## Definition
Hooke's law says the force from a spring is proportional to its extension: F = −k·x, where k is the spring constant and the minus sign means the force pulls back towards the equilibrium position.

## Equations
A mass on a spring oscillates in simple harmonic motion: x = A·cos(ωt) with ω = √(k/m), and period T = 2π·√(m/k). The maximum speed is A·ω, at the equilibrium position.

## Misconceptions
The period does not depend on the amplitude: a bigger stretch gives a faster motion over a longer distance, in the same time.
