## Definition
When two waves overlap, their displacements add (superposition). Where crests meet crests the waves reinforce (constructive interference); where crests meet troughs they cancel (destructive interference).

## Structure
Constructive interference happens where the path difference from the two sources is a whole number of wavelengths (nλ); destructive where it is (n + ½)λ. Diffraction is the spreading of a wave through a gap or round an obstacle; it is strongest when the gap is about the size of the wavelength. For a single slit of width a, the first dark direction is at sinθ = λ/a.

## Examples
Young's double-slit experiment (1801) showed light interfering, evidence that light behaves as a wave.

## Questions
What path difference gives a bright band? Why can you hear round a corner but not see round it?
