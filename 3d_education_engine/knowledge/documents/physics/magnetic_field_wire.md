## Definition
An electric current produces a magnetic field. Around a long straight wire the field lines are circles centred on the wire.

## Equations
B = μ0·I/(2π·r), with μ0 = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A. The field gets weaker with distance and stronger with current.

## Facts
Right-hand grip rule: grip the wire with your right thumb along the conventional current; your fingers curl in the direction of the field. Reversing the current reverses the field.

## Examples
Oersted discovered in 1820 that a current deflects a compass needle; electromagnets, motors and loudspeakers all depend on this effect.

## Questions
