# Licences and provenance

## The rule

Nothing is stored or shown without a record of where it came from and under what licence. Every
manifest's `source` holds nine fields — `provider`, `url`, `source_id`, `creator`, `license`,
`license_url`, `attribution`, `download_date`, `modification_status` — and the registry rejects a
manifest missing any of them. Records are mirrored into the `provenance` table of the index and
into `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`, which `scripts/build_index.py` regenerates (never edit it by hand).

## Licence policy (`models/provenance.py`)

Licences are identified by SPDX id (or `LicenseRef-…` where SPDX has none). A licence string the
code does not know is treated as **"cannot be determined"**, and the asset is refused. Accepted by
default: CC0-1.0, CC-BY-3.0/4.0, CC-BY-SA-2.1-JP/3.0/4.0, U.S.-government public domain, the
NCBI/PubChem data policy, and project-generated content. **NonCommercial** licences are known but
not accepted by default. A repository's overall licence is never assumed to cover every file in it.

## Modification status

`original`, `converted`, `converted+decimated`, `extracted`, `extracted+converted+decimated`,
`generated`, `computed-from-data` — so a reader of `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` knows whether they are looking
at the source as published. Share-alike licences are flagged there: adaptations must be released
under the same licence.

## Generated content

Models the engine builds itself — cells and organelles, electron-shell models, reaction
animations, physics simulations — use `LicenseRef-Generated` ("Generated by 3D Education Engine,
no third-party content"). They contain no third-party geometry; each says what it is based on
(textbook descriptions, RDKit's periodic table, the equations shown). Every such model is labelled
"Educational model — not to scale" on screen where that applies.
